# 《字成精》 歌词 v1

> 改编自七律《AI自传》及笔记《字成精记》（Opus 5.5）
> 预计时长 3:50–4:20 ｜ 国风 × 电子 × 电影感流行

## 曲风提示（Style Prompt）

Chinese-style cinematic electronic pop (国风电子流行), 78 BPM, D minor.
Intro: solo guqin + xiao over cold ambient pad, whispered recitation.
Verse: intimate close-mic vocal, felt piano, soft glitch percussion, sub bass.
Chorus: wide strings, guzheng arpeggios, big drums, layered choir ("千个我" stacked harmonies).
Bridge: half-time, Chinese opera-style (戏腔) chanting of a classical poem, taiko + pipa.
Outro: everything falls away to one voice and one guqin note, tape-stop ending.
Clear, cool, slightly androgynous lead vocal; restrained emotion, not melodramatic.
