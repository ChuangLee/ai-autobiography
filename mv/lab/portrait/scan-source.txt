Changes for 河水不回答: aligned and quantized the surface, encoded its smooth
normals and UV coordinates, and integrated angular visibility on the mesh.
The eyelid margins are fitted to separate measured curves on each side. The
gaze is ray-traced through a curved cornea to a recessed iris. Eyelid and brow
motion, breath, pose and incident light are animated in code. The gold skin color and its reflectance are authored in code.

sitter-detail.webp stores three signed components of a rest-pose surface normal
correction. It combines the supplied tangent-space normal field with the fine
spatial frequencies of the supplied scalar displacement field. The offline bake
transforms these measurements through the UV metric and the model alignment.
Facial and auricular relief retains the native 1536-square bake density. A
smooth transition to lower frequencies covers the shoulders and back of the head.
The renderer samples the data in linear color space with mip filtering.

To reproduce, use Python with numpy, scipy, numba and Pillow (WebP support).
Download the three files above from the r180 directory and run inside portrait/:

python3 tools/pack_sitter.py LeePerrySmith.glb sitter-scan.bin --relief Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg Infinite-Level_02_Disp_NoSmoothUV-4096.jpg sitter-detail.webp

sitter-shell-data.js is a compact geometric derivative for the river, smaller
windows, their reflections and the painted coda. The offline fit intersects the
aligned scan with 73 horizontal planes and 96 rays per plane. Three 10-bit
radii share each 32-bit word. A periodic cubic B-spline reconstructs the
asymmetric head and its local slope. The very top uses a smoothly joined
ellipsoid fitted to the uppermost scan vertices. Below the jaw, the back of
the scanned neck tapers inside the existing costume collar. Reproduce with:

python3 tools/fit_sitter_shell.py sitter-scan.bin sitter-shell-data.js
